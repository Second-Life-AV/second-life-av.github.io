Buying refurbished AV

Is refurbished AV as reliable as new?

Professional AV is built for years of daily use in corporate environments. Every unit we sell is fully tested to manufacturer specification by AV engineers and covered by a 12-month warranty, so if anything fails, we replace it.

What's the difference between Grade A, B and C?

Grades describe appearance only. Every grade passes the same functional tests. A is as new, B has light marks you won't see once installed, and C has visible wear but works perfectly. See the full standard.

Will it work with Microsoft Teams or Zoom?

Each listing states the platforms the device supports. We update to the latest supported firmware, and we'll tell you if a model is approaching end of support. Note that software licences and cloud subscriptions don't transfer with the hardware.

Do you provide VAT invoices?

Yes. Every order comes with a full VAT invoice, and prices can be shown ex or inc VAT anywhere on the site.

Can I buy in bulk or across several sites?

Yes. Trade accounts get tiered pricing, multi-room quotes, 48-hour stock holds and PO terms. For complete rooms, see our room bundles.

Why are some items also on eBay?

We use our eBay store for clearance, one-off lots and "spares or repair" items. Our graded, warrantied stock and trade pricing are on this site.

Warranty & delivery

What does the 12-month warranty cover?

Functional faults under normal use. We'll repair, replace or refund. It doesn't cover accidental damage, consumables such as lamps, or software licences. Full details.

Can I return something I don't need?

Business buyers can return items in their original condition within 30 days.

Can you install what I buy?

Yes. Our partner Workspace AV can design, install and support your rooms.

Selling & buy-back

What equipment do you buy?

Professional meeting-room AV: video bars and cameras, commercial displays, control processors and touch panels, DSPs, microphones, amplifiers, switchers and wireless presentation systems. See our credit bands.

How quickly will I get a price?

You'll get an indicative valuation within 48 hours of sending us your equipment list. The final offer follows our check-in audit.

Cash or credit: which is better?

Both are available. Trade-in credit against Second Life or new Workspace AV equipment is worth more than a cash payment.

How do you handle our data?

Control processors, codecs and booking panels can store network credentials and call histories. Every device is wiped to ADISA standard, and you receive an individual erasure certificate for each serial number.

What happens to items that don't work?

They're recycled free of charge through licensed WEEE channels and included in your disposition report.
