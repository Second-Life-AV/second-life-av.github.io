The greenest AV is the AV that already exists

Most of an electronic device's lifetime carbon footprint comes from making it, not running it. Every display, video bar or processor that gets a second life is one that doesn't need to be manufactured.

The circular model

Reuse first. Recycle only what's left.

  1. Recover

    Kit from corporate refreshes, office moves and surplus stock, collected and audited.

  2. Refurbish

    Repaired, cleaned, data-wiped and tested to manufacturer spec by AV engineers.

  3. Reuse

    Resold with a 12-month warranty to businesses that need reliable AV for less.

  4. Recycle

    Anything beyond economic repair goes to licensed WEEE recycling. Nothing goes to landfill.

Why it matters

Good for the planet. Good for the balance sheet.

Lower embodied carbon

Extending a device's life is one of the most effective ways to cut the Scope 3 emissions tied to your technology.

Less e-waste

E-waste is one of the world's fastest-growing waste streams. Professional AV is built to last, so let it.
