Second Life AV brand options

Three wordmark options, built on the Workspace AV lockup structure with the Second Life AV mark unchanged. Each comes in standard (navy on white) and reversed (white on navy).

Option 1

Classic

A direct sister to the Workspace AV logo: one colour, wide caps, spaced sub-line. Used on this preview site.

Option 2

Two-tone

"LIFE" in Second Life green: the same idea as the two-tone headlines, and it makes the "second life" message pop.

Option 3

Signal

Echoes the small signal icon from the Workspace AV wordmark, in green.

On dark backgrounds the mark sits on a white tile, because the supplied mark has navy strokes and a white background. A vector (SVG) version of the mark would allow a true reversed version, with white strokes and green towers, like the Workspace AV reversed logo.
