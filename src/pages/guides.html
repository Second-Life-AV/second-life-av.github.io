Refurbished AV grading explained

Grades describe how a unit looks, not how it works. Every unit we sell passes the same functional tests, so the real question is where it's going to live.

Grade A: as new

No visible marks from 50cm. Choose Grade A for anything your clients or board will see up close, such as boardroom touch panels, reception displays and executive offices.

Grade B: very good

Light marks you won't notice once it's installed. This is the sweet spot for most meeting rooms: the same performance as Grade A for a lower price.

Grade C: good

Visible cosmetic wear, but fully working. Perfect for kit nobody looks at, such as rack-mounted DSPs, amplifiers, extenders behind displays and ceiling-mounted equipment. It's the best value per pound.

What about warranty?

All three grades carry the same 12-month warranty. A lower grade means a lower price, not a lower standard.
