What's covered

Clear terms, no small-print surprises

  • Hardware faults and failures under normal use
  • Dead pixels on displays that appear after delivery
  • Ports, connectors and buttons that stop working
  • Return shipping on valid warranty claims

Not covered

  • Accidental damage, liquid damage or misuse
  • Consumables such as projector lamps and batteries
  • Software licences and cloud subscriptions
  • Items sold as "spares or repair"

How to claim

  1. Get in touch

    Email or call with your order number and the unit's serial number from its tested label.

  2. Quick diagnosis

    An engineer will help troubleshoot. Many issues are configuration or firmware, and we can fix them remotely.

  3. Repair or replace

    If the unit is faulty, we'll arrange collection and send a repaired or like-for-like replacement.
