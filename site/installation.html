What we install

From a single huddle room to a whole building

Video conferencing rooms

Teams, Zoom and Webex rooms with video bars, cameras, microphones and touch controllers, set up and tested on your platform.

Displays & LED walls

Commercial displays, video walls and LED, wall-mounted, cabled and calibrated.

Audio & DSP

Ceiling and table microphones, speakers, amplifiers and DSP, tuned to the room's acoustics.

Control systems

Crestron, Extron and AMX control, programmed so the room works with one touch.

Room booking

Booking panels and availability lights, linked to your calendar system.

Cabling & infrastructure

AV-over-IP, HDBaseT, racks and containment, installed neatly and documented.

How it works

Four steps to a working room

  1. Survey

    A site visit or remote call to understand your rooms, users, platform and budget.

  2. Design & quote

    A clear proposal built around tested second-life kit, with new equipment only where it makes sense.

  3. Install & commission

    Fitted, configured and tested by certified engineers, outside office hours if you need.

  4. Handover & support

    User training, documentation and optional support and maintenance packages.
