How it works

From quote to credit in six steps

  1. Tell us what you have

    Upload an asset list or photos, or just type the models and quantities. A rough idea is fine.

  2. Indicative price in 48 hours

    Based on our published credit bands and current demand for each model.

  3. We collect

    On a date that suits you, ideally install day, so old kit goes out as new kit comes in.

  4. Audit & certified erasure

    Each unit is checked in and wiped to ADISA standard, with an individual erasure certificate.

  5. Final offer

    Take cash, or take credit against Second Life or new Workspace AV kit. Credit is worth more.

  6. ESG pack

    WEEE certificate, disposition report and carbon statement within 10 working days.
