Flexible, reliable and planet-friendly AV

Second Life AV gives high-quality audio visual equipment a fresh start. It helps businesses stretch their budgets, reduce waste and build flexible setups without compromising on performance.

Our story

Born inside an AV integrator

Workspace AV designs, installs and supports meeting rooms, auditoriums and collaboration spaces for organisations in London and internationally. Every week we saw the same thing: perfectly good equipment coming off walls during upgrades, with nowhere to go but a skip.

Second Life AV is our answer. We take that kit back, refurbish it with the same engineers who install new systems, and make it available to businesses who need professional AV without the professional price tag.

Because we're integrators first, we know which models are reliable, which firmware matters and what a room really needs. We'll always recommend what's needed, never oversell, and focus on genuine value.
