Home / Grading & testing

Every unit works like new. The grade only describes how it looks.

Our standard is written by AV integration engineers, the people who install this kit for a living. It's simple: full functional testing for everything, and honest cosmetic grading.

Step one: function

Fully tested, working to manufacturer spec

No unit is graded or listed until it passes every relevant check.

  1. Sourcing & selection

    Kit comes from corporate decommissions and surplus stock that we've often installed ourselves, so we know its history.

  2. Professional refurbishment

    Technicians inspect, repair and clean each item, replace faulty components and restore it to full working order.

  3. Rigorous testing

    Audio and video quality, connectivity, network and PoE, controls and a soak test, logged against the serial number.

  4. Data & firmware

    Customer data and network credentials are erased to ADISA standard. Units are factory reset and updated to the latest stable firmware where it's supported.

  5. Grading & photos

    Cosmetic grade assigned under consistent lighting. Any visible marks are photographed for the listing.

  6. Packed & labelled

    Every unit leaves with a tested label, its serial number and grade, ready for install.

Step two: appearance

Our cosmetic grades

A

As new

No visible marks when viewed from 50cm. Often ex-demo, ex-stock or barely used. Original or equivalent packaging where available.

B

Very good

Light signs of use, such as fine scuffs or small marks, that won't be visible once the unit is installed.

C

Good

Visible cosmetic wear such as scratches or chips on the casing. Fully working, and ideal for racks, ceilings and behind-the-display kit.

For displays we also record panel hours and backlight uniformity where the device reports them. Units that fail any test are sold only as "spares or repair" on eBay, clearly labelled and without warranty.
